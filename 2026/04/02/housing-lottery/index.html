2026 Housing Lottery Guide

Everything you need to navigate Brown's three-day housing selection period.


Reporting by Rahul Sameer


Analysis and Website by Nikos Koss, Jasper Perlis, Rahul Sameer and Lucy Sun

↓
As April approaches, the Brown community begins to prepare for the long-awaited housing lottery. Responsible for determining where each student will be living for the upcoming school year, the housing process at Brown can be one of great anticipation as well as confusion, particularly for rising sophomores who have never gone through the process.

For this reason, The Herald decided to investigate housing trends to give readers more information on how last year’s lottery worked, and provide real-time updates of available dorms throughoutas students choose them during the three-day selection process.

This information has been synthesized into a campus map with each residence hall color-coded based on percentage availability. Students can filter this information based on their preferences, such as by room type and gender, and can control a slider that shows room availability over time.
